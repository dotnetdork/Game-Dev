- **Animator** — makes characters and objects *move* believably (a walk, a jump, an explosion).
- **Sound designer / composer** — the music and sound effects that make hits, coins, and wins feel great.
- **Level designer** — arranges the actual levels and maps so they're fair, fun, and get harder at the right pace.
