- **Game designer** — decides the *rules, mechanics, and feel*: what the player does and why it's fun. Writes it down so others can build it.
- **Programmer** — turns the design into working code: movement, scoring, collisions, bug fixes.
- **Artist** — makes it *look* like a game: characters, backgrounds, menus, effects.
