---
title: What Is "Juice"?
xp: 300
ai: full
summary: The polish that makes a game feel alive.
---
**Juice** is all the little feedback that makes a game feel great without changing the rules: a screen shake when you're hit, a pop when you score, a satisfying sound. Same game, way better feel.

```quiz
type: mcq
prompt: Which of these is "juice" (feel) rather than a rule?
options:
  - You lose a life when a bomb hits you
  - The screen shakes for a moment when a bomb hits you
  - The game ends at zero lives
answer: 1
feedback:
  - "That's a rule — it changes what happens."
  - "Right — the shake is feel, not a rule."
  - "Also a rule."
```
