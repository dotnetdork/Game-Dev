Time to build **your** game. You'll pick one of four starter genres and build it up over the next lessons:

- **Platformer** — run and jump between platforms (Mario-style).
- **Roguelike** — explore rooms, fight simple enemies, grab loot.
- **Arcade survival** — dodge or shoot as things get faster; chase a high score.
- **Top-down collector** — roam a small map and gather items before time runs out.

Pick the one that excites you most — any of them can be finished and shown off.
