# The v1 course — kept, not deleted

These are the 47 short lessons the course started as, before the rewrite into 5 modules of 4–5
fleshed-out lessons (see ../course.yaml).

They are here because they are the only record of what was covered, and several of them contain
challenges and phrasing worth stealing while the new lessons are written. Nothing loads them — the
app only reads what `course.yaml` lists.

Delete this folder once the 22 new lessons are finished and nothing in here is still wanted.
