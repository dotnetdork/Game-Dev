Most fun games mix three things: a clear **goal** (what am I trying to do?), quick **feedback** (did that work?), and a **reward** (nice, I'm getting somewhere!).

In a falling-stars game: catch the stars (goal), the score pops up instantly (feedback), your score climbs and new things appear (reward).
