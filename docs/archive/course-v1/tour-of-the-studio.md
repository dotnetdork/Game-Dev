Three tabs run the whole studio:

- **Learn** — the lesson you're reading now.
- **Code** — your game's files. **Save** keeps changes; **Run** plays them.
- **Play** — runs your game, with settings and a **console** at the bottom for debugging.

On the right is your **AI Assistant**. The icon toggles it between **Tutor** (explains things) and **Build** (changes your code).
