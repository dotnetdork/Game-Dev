Rules every screen follows (decided)

  • Ages 10–13, new to computers. Assume no knowledge of files, tabs, keyboard shortcuts or right-click.
  • Feels like a real game engine, runs like a course led by the AI.

Proposed rules (from the research, confirm)

  • Fits 1366×768 with nothing important hidden behind an icon.
  • Nothing needs dragging, resizing or right-click. Trackpads are hard at this age.
