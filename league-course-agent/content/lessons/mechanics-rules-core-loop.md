A **mechanic** is something the player *does* (jump, catch, shoot). A **rule** is what the game does in response. Chain them and you get the **core loop** — the short cycle players repeat.

Put a simple catching game's core loop in order:

```quiz
type: parsons
prompt: Order the core loop of a catch-the-stars game.
lines:
  - A star falls from the top
  - You move to catch it
  - The score goes up
  - A new star falls
```
