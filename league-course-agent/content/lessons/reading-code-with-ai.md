Here's the habit this whole course is building: ask the AI to make a change, then **read what changed and explain to yourself why it works**. The AI is fast, but you're the one making the decisions.

Try it now: in the **AI Assistant** on the right, ask it to *"make the player move faster,"* then open the **Code** tab and find the line it changed.
