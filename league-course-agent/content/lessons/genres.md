A **genre** is a family of games that play in similar ways: **platformers** (jump between platforms), **dodgers/arcade** (avoid or catch falling things), **collectors** (grab items around a map), **roguelikes** (rooms and enemies that change each run).

For a first game, smaller is better — a one-screen idea you can actually finish.
