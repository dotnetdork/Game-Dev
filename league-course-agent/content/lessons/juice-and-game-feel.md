**Juice** is the polish that makes a game feel alive: a little screen shake when you get hit, a pop when you score, a sound. None of it changes the rules — it changes how the game *feels*.

```quiz
type: mcq
prompt: Which of these is "juice" rather than a core rule?
options:
  - You lose a life when a bomb hits you
  - The screen shakes for a moment when a bomb hits you
  - The game ends at zero lives
answer: 1
```
